New in Minuted 2.1

What changed in this release.

Minuted shows you, live, what your meeting actually costs. Version 2.1 makes it free to start, keeps notes private, and gives the iPhone Duo a layout of its own.

Free to start

Minuted is now free. Meeting notes are a one-time unlock, with no subscription.

Private Cloud Compute

Notes use Private Cloud Compute when it is available. Turn it off in Settings to keep notes entirely on your device.

Made for iPhone Duo

On iPhone Duo, the inner display shows the full iPad layout, with your roster beside the meeting.

Three new app icons

Dusk, Ember and Ink, available as a one-time unlock in Settings.
