Most meeting tools assume your time is free. It isn't. A one-hour sync with four senior people can cost more than dinner for eight. Minuted turns that invisible cost into something you can feel: a number on your screen, climbing with every minute you stay in the room.

Quiet until it shouldn't be

Most meetings stay in the cool blue range and you barely notice the app is there. Then something drags on, the gradient starts to warm, and you remember why you installed it.

Agenda

Everything Minuted does, in the order you meet it.

  1. Build your roster

    Add the people you meet with and their hourly rate, or their salary and let Minuted work out the hour.

  2. Start from your calendar

    Minuted shows what is still left on your calendar today. Tap an event and the title fills in and the invitees are matched to your roster. Read-only, and entirely optional.

  3. Watch the live cost, in dollars

    The number on your screen updates every frame. No watching a timer and doing math in your head.

  4. Feel the background react

    A gradient shifts from cool blue through purple to urgent coral as the cost rises.

  5. Get an alert when it matters

    Pick thresholds, say $250 for a warning and $750 for an alarm, and Minuted taps you on the wrist when a meeting crosses them.

  6. Ask Siri, or just search

    Ask what you have spent on meetings this week. Find a past meeting from Spotlight. Start a Quick Meeting by voice, from Shortcuts, or from your watch.

  7. Keep the minutes

    Record a meeting and get structured notes back: what was decided, what was assigned, what actually mattered.

  8. Share the total

    Finish a meeting and get a card with the total, ready for Slack, Messages, or the next planning conversation.

  9. Read the history

    Every meeting is saved and tagged as a standup, a review, a one-on-one. Trends shows spend by week, month or quarter, and what you marked as not worth it.

  10. Glance at the week

    Home screen widgets keep this week's meeting spend in view, in small and medium sizes.

The minutes, written for you

Tap Record notes during a meeting. When it ends, the transcript becomes structured notes: a summary, decisions, action items with owners, and the questions still open.

The notes are written by Apple Intelligence, using Apple's Private Cloud Compute when it is available and the model on your device otherwise. Turn off Private Cloud Compute in Settings to keep everything on the device. The audio never leaves it, and nothing ever reaches us. Notes are a one-time unlock on devices with Apple Intelligence.
