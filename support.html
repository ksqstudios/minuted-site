Frequently asked questions

Which devices does Minuted run on?

iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch. One download covers all of them, the meeting notes unlock included, and your roster and history stay in step across every device signed in to your iCloud.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account to create and no sign-in. Minuted works the moment you open it. Syncing uses your own iCloud account, which you are already signed in to.

Does Minuted work offline?

Yes. Every part of the app works without a connection: starting meetings, tracking cost, and reading your history. The only thing that needs a connection is iCloud sync, which catches up on its own the next time you are online. Meeting notes use Apple's Private Cloud Compute when you are online, and are written on your device when you are not.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, and in your own private iCloud so it can sync between your devices. It is stored in the private database of your personal iCloud account, encrypted under your Apple ID.

KSQ Studios runs no servers, never receives a copy of your data, and cannot see it. See the privacy policy for the full detail.

How do I start a meeting from my calendar?

When you set up a meeting, Minuted shows the events still left on your calendar today. Tap one and the title fills in and the invitees are matched against your roster, so you can start tracking in a couple of taps.

Calendar access is read-only and entirely optional. Minuted never adds, edits, or deletes anything on your calendar, and the app works normally if you decline access.

How do meeting notes work?

Recording is something you start yourself on a meeting. Minuted never listens on its own. When the meeting ends, the transcript is turned into structured notes: the decisions, the action items, and the points that mattered.

Minuted is free, and meeting notes are a one-time in-app purchase on devices with Apple Intelligence: iPhone 15 Pro and later, and iPad and Mac with Apple silicon. Tap Record notes during a meeting, or open Settings, to unlock them. The purchase covers all your devices, and Restore Purchases in Settings brings it back on a new one.

The notes are written by Apple Intelligence, using Apple's Private Cloud Compute when it is available and the model on your device otherwise. You can turn off "Use Private Cloud Compute" in Settings to keep everything on your device. The audio never leaves your device either way, and nothing ever reaches KSQ Studios. Notes and transcripts are deleted along with the meeting they belong to.

Why do I need an hourly rate for someone on salary?

Minuted estimates hourly rate from annual salary by dividing by 2,080 (a standard working-hours-per-year figure). You can adjust this ratio in Settings.

The cost in the Live Activity isn't ticking. Is that a bug?

No, this is intentional. iOS doesn't allow Live Activities to update custom numbers continuously. Minuted shows the elapsed time ticking and the burn rate as a static reference. Open the app to see the exact live cost.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings and use either "Delete all meeting history," which clears your meetings and leaves your roster in place, or "Reset everything," which clears both. You will be asked to confirm, and neither can be undone.

Because your data syncs, deleting it removes it from your other devices too. Deleting the app from all of your devices also removes it.

Can I back up my data?

Your data already syncs through your own iCloud, so it is present on each of your devices rather than living on only one. If you have iCloud Backup switched on, Minuted's local data is also included in your device backup per your iCloud settings.

I have a feature request or found a bug. What should I do?

Email techsupport@ksqstudios.com. Bug reports are most useful when they include the device and OS version you are on (for example iPhone 17 Pro, iOS 26.6) and a description of what you expected versus what happened.
